## Welcome to The Picture of Two

We are a small indie studio making dark-fantasy games. Our first one is **Isekai Architect**.

**Isekai Architect** is an online card game for 2–5 players at one table. Each player builds a machine of cells and bets chips on it; the last player with chips wins. There is no "right" way to play: build quietly, break other machines, or bet big — but a bet only works on top of a machine that can back it.
