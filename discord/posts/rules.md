## Rules

**1. Be decent.** No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks. Argue about ideas, not people.
**2. No spam.** No ads, self-promotion or unsolicited DMs to members.
**3. Right place.** Bugs → #bug-reports, ideas → #ideas, everything else → #general.
**4. Keep it clean.** No NSFW, no piracy.
**5. Exploits go to us, not to everyone.** Found a way to break the game? Post it in #bug-reports, don't spread it.
**6. Moderation.** The Team's call is final. Questions about it — DM anyone with the Team role.

Breaking the rules means a warning, then a mute, then a ban.
